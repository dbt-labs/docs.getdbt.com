:::info Universal login

The recommended way to sign in to <Constant name="dbt_platform"/> is [https://login.dbt.com](https://login.dbt.com). After you enter and verify your email, you can see the accounts you have access to across instances and select an account to open.

You can also open your account using your **Access URL** from **Account settings** if you already know it (see [Access, Regions, & IP addresses](/docs/cloud/about-cloud/access-regions-ip-addresses)).

Account administrators can turn universal login on or off for the account with **Enable universal login url** in [Account settings](/docs/cloud/account-settings#enable-universal-login-url). When universal login is disabled for the account, users sign in using the account **Access URL** and their existing [SSO](/docs/cloud/manage-access/sso-overview) configuration, if applicable.

:::
