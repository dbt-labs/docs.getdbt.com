<Constant name="wizard" /> chats keep running in the background so if you start a chat, then switch tabs, open another page, or close your laptop &mdash; <Constant name="wizard" /> will keep working and have the results waiting when you're back.

- **Pick up where you left off:** Open any chat from your chat history to see its full conversation and current state. If it's still running, you see it live.
- **Check status at a glance:** Each chat in your history has a status badge: running, waiting for input, done, or failed.
- **Run several chats at once:** Start more than one chat across tabs and windows. Explore and Developer modes both run in the background.

:::note
Background chats are rolling out gradually and aren't available on single-tenant deployments yet.
:::
