## Terminology

The following terms are used throughout the private connectivity documentation:

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Native** | A private connectivity method built directly into the data platform by the vendor (for example, Snowflake PrivateLink, Databricks Private Link). |
| **Vendor** | The data platform provider (for example, Snowflake, Databricks, Redshift). |
| **Customer-provisioned** | Resources that you create and manage in your own cloud account, such as VPC Endpoint Services or Network Load Balancers. |
| **dbt-provisioned** | Resources that dbt Labs creates and manages on your behalf to establish the private connection. |
