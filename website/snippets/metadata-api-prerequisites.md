## Prerequisites

- You must have a <Constant name="dbt" /> [multi-tenant](/docs/platform/about-platform/tenancy#multi-tenant) or [single tenant](/docs/platform/about-platform/tenancy#single-tenant) account.
- You must be on a [Starter, Enterprise, or Enterprise+ plan](https://www.getdbt.com/pricing/).
- Your projects must be on a <Constant name="dbt" /> [release tracks](/docs/dbt-versions/dbt-release-tracks) or dbt version 1.0 or later. Refer to [Upgrade dbt version in Cloud](/docs/dbt-versions/upgrade-dbt-platform-version) to upgrade.
